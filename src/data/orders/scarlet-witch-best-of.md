# The Best of the Scarlet Witch: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to the best
Scarlet Witch comics, read as a reference and re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel
metadata and cannot be checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by
resolving each issue the outline names. The sections are the outline's, and they are kept in the
order it gives them rather than re-sorted into publication order.

Most of this outline names storylines rather than issues, so most of it is resolved from Marvel's
records. The Witch on Wundagore Mountain is the Chthon possession story, Avengers (1963) #185-187.
The two Vision and the Scarlet Witch limited series are the 1982 four-issue run and the 1985
twelve-issue run. Ragnarok Now is Uncanny Avengers (2012) #12-17. Scarlet Witch Volume 2 is the
2015 series, all fifteen issues. The Children's Crusade, Quicksilver: No Surrender and Avengers: No
Road Home are shipped complete, and Avengers Origins: Scarlet Witch & Quicksilver is a single
one-shot.

One row cannot be shipped, and is named here rather than dropped silently. The outline's Scarlet
Witch Volume 1 is the 1994 four-issue mini-series in which Wanda trains with Agatha Harkness, and
Marvel's series index has no record of it: the only series Marvel calls Scarlet Witch are the 2015,
2023 and 2024 runs. Its section is gone rather than filled with a guess.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
