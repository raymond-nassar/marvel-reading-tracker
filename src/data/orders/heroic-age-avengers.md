# The Heroic Age, an Avengers Reading List: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to Avengers
trade collections, covering the Heroic Age titles of 2010 to 2012, read as a reference and
re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and
cannot be checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by resolving each
issue the outline names. The sections are kept in the order the outline gives them.

The outline groups its trades under era headings and this checklist has one level of section, so
each trade is its own section here. The sequence is unchanged.

A list holds an issue once, and this outline overlaps itself twice at a trade boundary. Avengers
#13 is the last issue of the volume listed as #7-13 and the first of the Fear Itself volume listed
as #13-17, so it is shipped in the earlier section and the Fear Itself section starts at #14. Secret
Avengers #6 is the last issue of the volume listed as #1-6 and the first of the one listed as #6-12,
so the second section starts at #7.

Secret Avengers #21.1 is a real issue and is not a duplicate of #21. It shipped between #21 and #22,
which is where the outline puts it and where it is here. Avengers #12.1 is the same kind of issue
and is included for the same reason, inside the range listed as #7-13, although the outline does not
name it.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
