# Dark Reign, an Avengers Reading List: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to Avengers
trade collections, covering the Avengers path through Dark Reign, read as a reference and
re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and cannot be
checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by resolving each issue the
outline names. The sections are kept in the order the outline gives them.

The outline groups its trades under era headings and this checklist has one level of section, so
each trade is its own section here. The sequence is unchanged.

The gap in Dark Avengers is deliberate and it is the outline's. It lists #1-6, then #9-12, then
#13-16, and gives #7-8 their own place later as the Utopia crossover, which is where they belong to
read: Utopia interrupts the run. Those two issues are shipped in that later section, after the
one-shot that opens the crossover.

That Utopia row asks for tie-ins it does not name. The crossover runs through Uncanny X-Men as well,
and this is an Avengers list, so what is shipped is the opening one-shot and the two Dark Avengers
issues. The X-Men issues are named here rather than shipped.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
