# There is Only Doom: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to the best
Doctor Doom comics, read as a reference and re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel
metadata and cannot be checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by
resolving each issue the outline names. The sections are the outline's, and they are kept in the
order it gives them rather than re-sorted into publication order.

The outline labels several Fantastic Four rows as volume 2, and Marvel's metadata has no such
volume for any of them: #116, #195-200, #241-250, #258-260, #278, #287-288 and #318-319 are all
Fantastic Four (1961), and they carry that title here. The one volume label that does hold is
Fantastic Four (v3) #67, which is the 1998 series.

A list holds an issue once, so rows repeating an issue listed elsewhere are not shipped again. The
outline's Early Doom section names Fantastic Four #5 and Prisoners of Doctor Doom, which is a story
inside that same issue, and #5 is already in the origin section; nothing else was in Early Doom, so
the section is gone. Fantastic Four #278 appears under both the origin stories and the Secret Wars
II tie-ins. Fantastic Four #258-260 appears under Werner von Doom and twice more in the antihero
section, once as #258 alone.

Four rows carry no issue numbers and are resolved to the runs they name. Books of Doom is the 2005
six-issue mini-series. Infamous Iron Man is the complete twelve-issue run. Secret Wars (2015) is
the nine-issue main series, not the event's tie-ins. The Winter Soldier row names a Doom team-up in
the Brubaker and Guice run, which is Winter Soldier (2012) #1-14.

The section on Doom keeping the Beyonder's power names three things, and they are one issue. Marvel
has a single record answering that question, the 2008 What If? one-shot, so it is shipped once;
Secret Wars #10 is already in the 1984 section.

Two rows cannot be shipped at all, and they were the whole of one section, which is therefore gone.
Marvel's index of Astonishing Tales (1970) runs #7, then #9 to #24, then #26, with no #8, so the
Cynthia von Doom issue is not available to link. Marvel's only record of Doctor Strange & Doctor
Doom: Triumph and Torment is a trade collection rather than an issue. Marvel Graphic Novel #27,
Emperor Doom, is not in Marvel's catalogue either, only #1 of that series is, so that section is
gone as well. The outline's Secret Wars III heading is its own invention, Marvel has no such event,
and the issues under it are kept where the outline puts them.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
