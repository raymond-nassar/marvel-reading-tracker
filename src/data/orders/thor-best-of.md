# The Best Thor Comics: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to the best
Thor comics, read as a reference and re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and
cannot be checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by resolving each
issue the outline names. The sections are the outline's, and they are kept in the order it gives
them rather than re-sorted into publication order.

The outline names Journey into Mystery #126 as the issue where the title becomes Thor. That is the
change itself: Journey into Mystery ends at #125, and #126 is the first issue of Thor (1966). Marvel
records it that way and so does this list.

The outline's volume labels are resolved to Marvel's series records. Thor Vol. 2 #85 is Thor (1998)
#85, Vol. 4 #1-8 is Thor (2014), and Vol. 5 #1-16 is Thor (2018). Mighty Thor #1-23 and #705 are one
series, Mighty Thor (2015), which restored the legacy numbering partway through its run.

A list holds an issue once. Thor #158-159, listed as the divine origin of Donald Blake, sits inside
the #154-167 range listed above it, so it is not shipped again. King Thor appears in the outline
twice, in the God Butcher section as the Gorr sequel and again in the title of the final section; it
is shipped once, where the outline first lists it.

Three rows carry no issue numbers and are resolved to the runs they name: Thor: Blood Oath and
Stormbreaker: The Saga of Beta Ray Bill are both 2005 six-issue mini-series, and War of the Realms is
the 2019 six-issue main event series rather than its tie-ins.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
