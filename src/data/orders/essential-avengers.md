# Essential Avengers: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to Avengers
trade collections, covering the Epic Collections and story arcs worth reading first, read as a
reference and re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and cannot be checked
against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by
resolving each issue the outline names. Every section is Avengers (1963), the outline's ranges are
taken as given, and they are already in publication order.

Nothing in the outline overlaps and nothing was dropped, so this list is the outline exactly. The
gaps between sections are the outline's own: it picks ten runs out of the first 285 issues rather
than covering them continuously.

One issue is here that the outline's ranges do not spell out. Marvel numbers Avengers #1.5 inside
the opening range, between #1 and #2, and it is included on that basis even though it was published
in 1999 as a retrospective issue.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
