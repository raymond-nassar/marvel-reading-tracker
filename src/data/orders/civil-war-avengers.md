# Civil War, an Avengers Reading List: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to Avengers
trade collections, covering the Avengers path through Civil War, read as a reference and
re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and cannot be
checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by resolving each issue the
outline names. The sections are kept in the order the outline gives them.

The outline groups its trades under era headings and this checklist has one level of section, so
each trade is its own section here rather than being folded into a single Post-Civil War Split
heading. The sequence is unchanged.

Two rows carry no issue numbers and are resolved to what they name. The core event is Civil War
(2006) #1-7, the seven-issue main series rather than its tie-ins. New Avengers: Illuminati is listed
as #0-5, and Marvel has no #0: it has a 2006 one-shot and a separate 2008 five-issue mini-series, so
the one-shot is shipped first in the place the outline gives #0.

The Thunderbolts run is here because the outline asks for it, and it says why: it is not an Avengers
title, but Warren Ellis and Mike Deodato's #110-121 is what makes Dark Avengers make sense.

Marvel's metadata holds two separate records for Avengers/Invaders #2, alike but for their ids. A
list holds an issue once, so the lower id is the one here.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
