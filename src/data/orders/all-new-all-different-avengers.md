# All-New, All-Different Avengers: Issue-by-Issue Reading Checklist

Compiled by hand for this project from a reader's outline of Comic Book Herald's guide to Avengers
trade collections, covering the All-New, All-Different Avengers era, read as a reference and
re-expressed here. It is not derived from Marvel metadata and cannot be
checked against it; only the issue ids and titles come from Marvel, by resolving each issue the
outline names. The sections are the trades the outline lists, kept in its order.

Three rows carry no issue numbers, and each is resolved to what the surrounding rows leave. A-Force
Vol. 2 is recorded in the outline as title only; Vol. 1 collects A-Force (2016) #1-5 and the series
ran ten issues, so Vol. 2 is #6-10. Occupy Avengers Vol. 1 is listed without numbers and Vol. 2
collects #5-9, so Vol. 1 is #1-4. Great Lakes Avengers is listed without numbers and ran seven
issues, all of which are here.

The gap in All-New, All-Different Avengers is the outline's. It lists #1-6 and then #9-12, skipping
the two Standoff tie-ins, and that is left as it asked.

Rage of Ultron is an original graphic novel rather than a numbered issue, and Marvel indexes it as a
single unnumbered issue of its own series, which is how it is shipped here.

Four issues are here that the outline's ranges do not spell out. Marvel numbers Avengers #1.1, #2.1,
#3.1 and #4.1 inside the range listed as #1-6, so they are in that section. Marvel also holds two
records each for Avengers #5 and #1.1, alike but for their ids; a list holds an issue once, so the
lower id is the one here.

Because the selection cannot be verified against Marvel, the order says so on its catalog card
rather than presenting it as Marvel's own. See
[the data provenance record](../../../docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for what that means for reuse.
